What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. In practical terms, AI encompasses various technologies, including machine learning, natural language processing, and robotics.
